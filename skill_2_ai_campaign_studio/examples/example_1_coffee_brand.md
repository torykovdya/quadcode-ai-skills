## Input

"Minimalist coffee brand for young professionals. Tone: calm, premium, natural."

---

## Step 1 — Brand Analysis

- **Brand Personality:** Calm, premium, natural
- **Target Audience:** Young professionals (25–40) who value quality, aesthetics, and mindful morning rituals
- **Campaign Goal:** Brand awareness and emotional connection — audience should feel that this coffee elevates their daily routine
- **Emotional Tone:** Serene, grounded, sophisticated, understated
- **Key Visual Metaphors:** Morning light, ceramic texture, negative space, slow rituals

---

## Step 1.5 — Differentiation Strategy

**What coffee brands typically do:**
- Warm brown tones, latte art, rustic wood surfaces
- Busy café environments
- Close-ups of coffee being poured (very overused)
- Dark roast drama (black backgrounds, steam)

**What this campaign should avoid:**
- Latte art imagery
- Warm orange/brown palette (too expected)
- Crowded café scenes

**Unique visual territory to own:**
- Architectural minimalism — the cup as a design object
- Morning light as the hero element, not the coffee
- Muted sage + warm white palette (unusual for coffee)
- Stillness and silence as brand values

---

## Step 1.6 — Business Visualization Strategy

| Service/Product | Visual Representation | Recurring Symbol |
|----------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Premium coffee | Single ceramic cup | The cup |
| Morning ritual | Soft natural light | Light rays |

---

## Step 2 — Creative Direction

**Campaign Concept:** "The quietest part of your day."

**Campaign Narrative:** Three images that trace a single morning — the environment, the craft, the feeling. Each image slows the viewer down.

**Color Palette:**
- `#F4F1EA` — Warm Paper White
- `#4A443D` — Deep Espresso
- `#8C9A8B` — Muted Sage
- `#D2B48C` — Soft Sand
- `#E8E0D5` — Linen

**Lighting Style:** Soft, directional natural light. Window light with gentle shadows.

**Composition Style:** Negative space dominant. Subject occupies maximum 40% of frame.

### Style Anchor

```
Minimalist lifestyle photography aesthetic, soft natural window lighting,
warm paper white and muted sage color palette (#F4F1EA, #8C9A8B, #4A443D),
shallow depth of field, negative space composition, calm and premium atmosphere,
high-end editorial quality, 1:1 aspect ratio
```

---

## Step 2.5 — Brand System

- **Typography Direction:** Clean sans-serif, high letter-spacing, light weight (Inter Light or Montserrat Thin)
- **Iconography Style:** Single-line, minimal, no fill
- **Brand Voice:** Understated, confident, never loud — speaks quietly and means it
